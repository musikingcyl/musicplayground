One Chord, Many Smiles 🎶

Play open strings, try your first C chord, and record a little tune together. No ukulele needed!

1. Play the strings

Tap a string or use keys 1–4

Ukulele strings are tuned G – C – E – A. Tap each one to hear it.

2. Learn one chord: C

GCEA

Place one finger on the 3rd fret of the A string (the string closest to the floor when holding your ukulele). Leave the other three strings open.

Then gently strum all four strings. You have played a C major chord!

3. Record your music

Press record, play some strings or chords, then stop and listen. Recording captures sounds played inside this app, not your voice or a real ukulele.
